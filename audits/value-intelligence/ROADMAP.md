The Director's six mandatory corrections A–F are preserved below (the deliverable calls them five; none is omitted).

## A. Measurable AI contribution

Before any AI coefficient is used, predefine the independent raw-football statistical baseline, exact prompt/model/feature versions and base-only comparator. Fit and validate any mapping only on admissible football data. Compare proper scores, calibration, support/coverage and ablations on identical later fixtures; register meaningful improvement/nondegradation gates and report dependence-aware uncertainty. Feature effects are predictive associations, not causal player/tactical claims. No manual boost or synthetic coefficient becomes a live parameter. Stage 1 accepts only mathematical rate inputs and supplies no learned mapping or AI workflow.

## B. Realistic point-in-time acquisition before fitting

First conduct a read-only coverage/provenance and storage-budget assessment under separate approval. Inventory existing trusted receipts and identify actual missing fields/sources; do not reconstruct historical availability from today's mutable rows. Predeclare a prospective roster, competition scope, complete history, target identity mapping, cutoff and observation/correction fields. Capture full football evidence before any issue, store genuine server receipt/effective/played times and exact prompt/request/response identities, then append genuine 90-minute outcomes later. Preserve missing/abstained/failed rows. Independent OpenAI and Gemini packets must be equivalent and structurally omit odds, engine/other-model outputs and target post-cutoff data.

Without paid extraction authorization, begin with offline provenance/feature audits and synthetic pipeline tests only. Do not promise a cheap retrospective AI training set: model memory/outcome leakage and absent first receipts can keep historical extraction exploratory. Evaluate actual eligible throughput, dependent effective sample, annotation reliability and model-call/storage costs before requesting a financially feasible collection protocol. Existing lifetime holds are not reusable training credit.

Only after a real cohort exists should independently ordered baseline-training, AI-mapping, calibration, policy-development and untouched validation periods be registered. Missing/inadequate data means no fit. A short one-fixture pilot cannot validate coefficients. No data acquisition integration, collection scheduler or coefficient estimation is implemented in Stage 1.

## C. Genuinely usable quotes

Stage 2 needs separate approval to inspect the collector/account/coverage/catalog/terms, then demonstrate fixture/book/market/selection/line/rules identity, upstream update and trusted receipt clocks, suspension/tradability, commission and operator accessibility. Named per-book `prices` is a useful retained structure but not proof a quote remains executable. Reject missing/stale/unknown-status/unmatched/rules-incompatible prices; do not silently replace a suspended latest quote with an older one. The actual provider cadence may make actionable freshness unattainable; use delayed research or skip rather than weaken gates. No bookmaker integration or subscription is included here.

## D. Predeclared model and ranking policies

Before later selection, freeze the model family and uncertainty construction, allowed model/prompt versions, supported competitions/strata, fourteen-market universe, accessible book policy, quote TTL/rules, all selection thresholds/risk preference/tie-breaks and disagreement behavior. Keep per-model results and audit every rejected candidate. Do not choose whichever AI or policy reports the largest apparent EV. Any cross-model mixture or consensus policy needs separately learned/frozen out-of-sample evidence and prospective testing, not ad hoc averaging or confidence inflation. Stage 1 implements no ranking function.

## E. Actionable selector stays disabled

There is **no executable value selector or public recommendation path** in Stage 1. New objects explicitly report research-only/unvalidated status. Unknown or inadequate calibration, parameter uncertainty, source support or quote verification must remain a blocker in later admission. Mathematical normalization/tail accuracy is not football forecast calibration. Existing guards and the actual-Sunday execution restriction remain untouched; any future-date workflow needs separate design/tests/authorization. No request, financial reservation, ledger mutation or research checkpoint write is introduced by these modules.

## F. No fictional policy defaults

The review's illustrative ROI/edge/risk-reference/error-allowance values are not copied into executable selector defaults. Synthetic ranking examples are never real picks. Stage 1 tail tolerance/resource limits are **numerical settings only**; they confer no probability-confidence level, football uncertainty estimate or value threshold. Real policy thresholds need development-data justification and untouched prospective evaluation before any activation. Until then the correct actionable behavior remains unavailable/skip, not a forced recommendation.
