# Proposed bounded receipt-feasibility pilot — not activated

This is an engineering/data-quality proposal, **not** a statistically powered accuracy study, resource-creation instruction or permission to modify a hosted project. No real collection, publisher/collector execution, provider request or learned football model is activated by Phase 7.

## Least-cost evidence path

The retained historical archive cannot be made historically receipt-verified by assigning old `captured_at`/`updated_at` values to new receipts. The least-cost scientifically valid alternative is:

1. Obtain a separately authorized, private read-only export of the current complete required archive. Ingest it under a genuine receipt **now**. It may bootstrap future issues after that receipt, not old forecasts.
2. After private source/staging review, passively record the payloads already collected by the existing collector, with an independently approved source identity, clock, immutable receipt ledger and signing trust root. Reuse an existing adequate private execution environment and plain isolated PostgreSQL; no third Supabase project, subscription or paid service is proposed.
3. Capture exact original publication identity and candidate input membership at issue, eligible bookmaker observations before it, and final/corrected outcomes afterward. Keep private backend source and signing/provider credentials out of this public repository.

This route may require no **additional** provider requests if current licensed collection already supplies all required inputs. That assumption has not been verified. If field coverage, upstream inventory, licensing, request quotas or an adequate durable private runtime are missing, stop and review them; do not silently add calls, pay for resources, backfill provenance or alter production. Codex workspace persistence is not a reliable unattended production collector.

## Exact proposed default scope

- **Seven consecutive UTC issuance days**, chosen and registered in advance, followed by a **seven-day label tail** after the issue window. No efficacy-driven extension.
- One predeclared competition; league 39 is an illustrative adult-90-minute scope whose availability/calendar/licensing must be verified before approval. It is selected for feasibility, not because a small historical subgroup looked profitable.
- At most the **first two original issues per UTC day**, ordered by authoritative issue time and fixture ID. Maximum 14 originals; actual volume may be much lower. Selection must not depend on outcome, confidence, xgEdge availability, market disagreement or a successful capture. Keep scope counts and every selected failure in the roster.
- Preserve the complete existing M14 dependency history and immutable manifests for those issues. The archive can include many nonselected fixtures and seasons. Continue complete required competition history through the final issue; do not collect only successful/selected teams' results if doing so would omit required context.
- One valid immutable pre-issue bookmaker observation per selected original. If none was actually received in time, mark it unpaired; no later replacement. This cap is an acquisition schedule, not proof that an observation will be available before the existing publisher's issue. That timing compatibility needs private staging verification.
- Outcome schedule: first final-result observation once full time is verified, plus correction checks at **+24 hours** and **+7 days** after that final event. Retain all changed versions. Changes received after the fixed label cutoff are recorded but cannot rewrite the frozen evaluation label.
- No new prediction enrollment, pre-issue odds or feature-history refresh after the issue window. The tail observes only already selected jobs' outcome/correction status. A +7-day check falling beyond the fixed tail does not extend the study; record it as unavailable at the cutoff. Late/unsettled fixtures remain pending/void under the existing policy.
- No prediction engine or paid AI activation. The default 14-original maximum is below even the synthetic fitter's 20-row threshold. This pilot therefore does not promise or require a real coefficient fit.

## Capacity, stop and rejection rules

The unchanged research admission ceiling is **262144000 bytes (250 MiB)**. The proposed planning reserve is 25%; the operational enrollment stop is **200 MiB of actual combined research/training/shadow relations and indexes**. Preserve enough space for existing jobs' labels and repair receipts. The SQL cap is conservative admission accounting, not an OS-enforced physical disk quota.

Preflight must measure the actual complete bootstrap and current existing research use, not assume an empty database or these illustrative field counts. Require both estimated raw peak <=200 MiB and reserved planning peak <=250 MiB. If a larger archive, required field, changed payload size or increased diversity fails either condition, **do not launch** that scope. Do not truncate evidence to obtain a pass.

If actual growth reaches the stop, halt enrollment with an immutable administrative stop reason. Preserve the already selected roster and every failure, and finish only the reserved label/repair work that remains possible. A halted or incomplete pilot is reported as such; it does not become a favourable smaller accuracy sample. Never delete study inputs to continue unnoticed or increase the cap automatically.

`pilot.mjs` prices 7/14/28 issuance days plus the fixed seven-day tail. Assumptions are explicit: two issues/day; complete new competition inventory allowance of 30 fixtures/week; 36 retained fields/new fixture as an **estimate**, not a field cap; two correction checks with 5% changed values; one pre-issue odds observation/selected prediction; three label revisions; eight operational receipts/job; compact samples/artifact; complete history manifests. Manifest costs use the conservative diverse-team Phase 4 slope, scaled with bootstrap size and history growth. Repeat-share sensitivity is allowed only as an assumption, not a reason to omit data.

For a 68,400-observation bootstrap (illustratively 1,900 fixtures ×36 fields), estimated planning storage is about **199 MiB at seven days**, **267 MiB at 14 days**, and **411 MiB at 28 days**. A 99,000-observation bootstrap already requires about **286 MiB for seven days**. These are estimates using retained synthetic byte slopes, not measured football upper bounds. The aggregate results include raw observations, immutable manifests/original snapshots, compact training examples, shadow forecasts, outcome labels, bookmaker observations, indexes and operations. WAL, catalogues, containers, private backups/export copies and unbounded bloat require separate existing filesystem capacity; they are not hidden in the database estimate.

Reject/stop the receipt pilot for unapproved source identity or clock, forged/backdated receipts, missing upstream fixture inventory, altered original probabilities, incomplete required membership, unexplained correction ordering, permission leakage, unreconciled selected jobs, quota exhaustion or inability to finish its label tail. Missing xG itself is a measured availability result; it must cause an explicit candidate abstention, not a fictitious zero or omission from the roster.

## Pilot endpoints

Report selected/excluded-by-predeclared-scope counts, immutable original capture rate, receipt-qualified feature coverage, 90-minute label availability/correction rate, paired pre-issue market coverage, failures/repairs, exact-fallback availability, complete input counts, bytes by component and latency. Label uncertainty and small denominators prominently. Suggested **engineering** feasibility goals for a subsequent design review are >=99% timely original/candidate-input capture, >=80% qualified feature and market coverage, no unexplained immutable-lineage mismatch, no unresolved source/permission defect, and capacity below the fixed stop. With <=14 examples, one failure can materially change percentages; no broad service-quality claim follows.

There is no automatic continuation or model promotion if those goals appear met. A development cohort with enough trustworthy data, an independently learned/frozen coefficient and a new untouched evaluation period would still be required.

## Proposed later learned-model evaluation protocol

Before real fitting or evaluation, independently approve the actual receipt authority, complete inventory, frozen code/configuration and a feasible sample-size/inference plan. Existing 2025–26 M14/October audit holdouts are development evidence, not fresh tests. A new development cohort may train the one coefficient; its final fitting dataset export and all selected outcome receipts must precede fit. A server-registered artifact/protocol receipt must precede every issue in a genuinely new evaluation window. No new hyperparameter search, refit or repeated efficacy peeking on that window.

Compare three forecasts on identical fixtures: exact original probabilities; the independently frozen learned xgEdge candidate; and de-vigged, receipt-eligible bookmaker probabilities. Score candidate failures by exact original fallback in intention-to-forecast analysis; also show successful-only diagnostics. Keep all original/scorable and market-paired denominators separate, including pending/void/missing-capture/ambiguous-90-minute counts. A candidate cannot gain by silently losing difficult fixtures. Settlement uses valid regulation-time outcomes, original kickoff and the existing 48-hour rule; revisions are selected only by the fixed receipt cutoff.

Proposed **co-primary** score gates, to be frozen and independently reviewed before that study:

| Candidate minus reference | Material point-estimate requirement |
|---|---:|
| Brier versus original | <=-.005 |
| Brier versus market on the same market pairs | <=-.002 |
| Log loss versus original | <=-.005 nats |
| Log loss versus market on the same market pairs | <=-.002 nats |

For all four, require one-sided **98.75% upper bounds below zero**, a Bonferroni family-error proposal of .05 across four comparisons. Require an original-versus-candidate paired-subset consistency check, adequate preregistered coverage, no meaningful calibration or major-group harm and intact receipt/label audit. Market-only blending cannot pass the market superiority gates. Boundary beta solutions, unidentifiable inputs, low coverage, data-driven endpoint changes or insufficient power reject promotion or yield inconclusive evidence.

The Phase 7 code reports descriptive paired 95% week/competition intervals; those **do not implement the above confirmatory gate**. Before any accuracy study, an appropriate dependence-aware estimator and its multiplicity/coverage calibration must be frozen and verified on independent development/simulated data, including shared teams and time dependence. The exact study scope determines the estimator; one small competition cannot be treated as 20 independent competition clusters. No confirmatory study may start with this unresolved.

For scale only, the retained Phase 5 original-versus-market Brier SD was about .18461. A normal 80%-power planning calculation with a 98.75% one-sided threshold needs roughly **13,000 independent pairs for a .005 effect** and **81,000 for .002**; an assumed dependence factor of two doubles those counts. New candidate-relative variance may differ, and log-loss/subgroup precision can require more. These are feasibility warnings, not achieved power or a validated study design. Fourteen possible pilot originals cannot support such a claim. At one competition's fixture volume and the current budget, a powered bookmaker-relative accuracy study is not presently established as feasible.
