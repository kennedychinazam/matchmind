# Proposed private prospective protocol — not activated

Status: design for review. No chosen candidate, registration, scheduled collection, live-model retraining or deployment. No paid AI or provider requests. This design does not alter the public publisher, engine, picks, Smart Bet or UI. PR #1 remains separate and draft.

## Registration and eligible evidence

1. Select at most **one primary candidate** after independent review of development evidence. Register its exact source/configuration hash, training rows/evidence hashes, training cutoff, fixed parameters, input requirements, missingness/fallback policy, scorer, target sample, end rules and analysis code. Keep other candidates exploratory; a changed version starts a new future study, never inherits the old test as untouched evidence.
2. Start no earlier than the latest of 1 November 2026 UTC, candidate freeze plus 24 hours, and a verified receipt-capable collection start. Start approval and actual receipt capability are prerequisites. A date-based `prospective` label in the old helper is insufficient. `shadowTimingEligible` checks timing only; it does not authenticate timestamp origin, receipts, version hashes, publication identity or immutable storage.
3. Store each shadow result privately against an immutable original prediction ID, original output probabilities/pick/kickoff/build, original issuance timestamp, candidate version, server capture timestamp, evidence IDs/hashes and feature hash. Never update a public prediction or use a shadow row as the winning publication. Separate private schema, roles, RLS/grants and authenticated operator-only export. Reject anonymous/ordinary application users. Credentials and private collector/backend source remain outside public GitHub.
4. Eligible odds must have authoritative server receipt **strictly before issuance**, valid source identity, prices >1, overround 1.00–1.30, positive kickoff lead and receipt age <=24 hours. Record provider event time separately; it cannot replace receipt. Reject unknown/backdated receipts, late imports and post-issue reconstruction. Select latest eligible receipt with deterministic ID tie-break; freeze source payload and decision, including exclusions. Use the same immutable odds on both references/candidates. Prefer contemporaneous named-book prices for a cleaner benchmark; do not silently change aggregation convention after study start.
5. Immutable history/feature inputs require authoritative receipt before issue and the historical event before its receipt; use the existing feature builder. Candidate fitting uses only outcome labels whose authoritative settlement receipt predates the fit. The old `updated_at` proxy is not acceptable for confirmatory training. If needed inputs are not provable, do not manufacture the missing study rows from mutable history.
6. Shadow generation uses only issue-time inputs and is persisted before kickoff with an enforceable deadline (target <=60 seconds after original issue and always before kickoff). Quarantine late outputs. Prefer issue-transaction capture of the candidate's inputs/results; any asynchronous approach needs a separate provenance review. The asynchronous receipt/outbox architecture remains a **future option**, not implementation in this PR.

## Outcomes, failures and denominators

- Preserve the existing 90-minute settlement and 48-hour late-void rules. Append versioned settlement receipts/corrections, freeze the analysis cutoff and state unresolved/ambiguous FT handling. Never overwrite prior labels to conceal a correction. No outcome-dependent selection.
- Primary denominator: all eligible issued original predictions in the fixed study window. Market-paired denominator: the predetermined authoritative-odds subset of those exact fixtures. Score candidate and original on all, and candidate/original/market on the identical paired subset. Report market coverage by league, lead time and calendar week. Do not replace odds with later observations.
- Record every capture/missing-input/permission/quota/deadline failure privately with original prediction ID, safe reason code, attempt count and repair status; alerts and dead-letter review are mandatory. Optional research failures must preserve original publication. Repairs must use already persisted pre-issue receipts; otherwise the snapshot is permanently unavailable. No warning-only monitoring or reconstruction from today's data.
- Predeclare identity fallback on candidate failure for intention-to-forecast scoring, and report successful-shadow-only scores separately. Missing odds remain explicitly unpaired, not a candidate win. Coverage gate proposal: >=80% eligible predictions have trustworthy market pairs, >=99% on-time candidate availability, failure rate <=1%. If unmet, study is inconclusive for broad promotion; do not improve coverage by dropping difficult competitions after inspection.
- Diagnostic volume/error dashboards may be inspected without outcomes. Blind candidate outcomes until fixed target/end. No repeated efficacy peeking, winner selection or early release. At a prespecified administrative deadline, an underpowered study reports inconclusive, rather than extending based on favourable scores. Safety/correctness reviews may halt collection.

## Proposed acceptance gates, fixed before collection

Definitions: summed three-class Brier (0–2, lower better), natural-log loss (clip zero at 1e-12 consistently); each difference is candidate minus reference on the same fixtures. Top-choice accuracy and calibration are secondary. Original probabilities are preserved at stored precision; no identity renormalization.

| Gate | Proposed requirement |
|---|---|
| Original-reference superiority | Mean paired Brier change <=-.005 on all eligible originals and also negative on the market-paired subset |
| Market-reference superiority | Mean paired Brier change <=-.002 on the same eligible market pairs |
| Uncertainty for both primary comparisons | Both one-sided 97.5% upper bounds <0; Bonferroni family error <=.05 for the two primary tests |
| Log-loss protection | One-sided 95% upper bound for change <+.002 versus each reference on its declared cohort |
| Coverage and provenance | Meet the above timing, receipt, immutable output, availability and audit gates; no unknown late imports counted as available |
| Robustness | No material aggregate or major-subgroup harm under the preregistered analyses below; no sole small-subgroup promotion |

The primary original comparison uses all eligible originals; the paired-original subset is an additional consistency guard. Both comparator improvements are required; market-only blending fails the market superiority gate by construction. The point-estimate material thresholds and intervals against zero are distinct requirements; they do not claim a confidence interval wholly beyond the material margin.

Before freeze, specify and validate a suitable multiway dependence-aware analysis for shared competitions, teams and time blocks using development/synthetic data, without future study outcomes. League and issuance-week resampling in `validation.mjs` is a descriptive starting point, **not** the completed confirmatory estimator. Suggested method evaluation: block/multiway cluster bootstrap with whole paired fixture losses and a season/time-block sensitivity, verifying coverage under simulated team/time shocks. If its assumptions or cluster counts are untenable, evidence is inconclusive. Require at least 12 issuance weeks and 20 competitions plus the power-based fixture target; these minima alone do not assure valid inference. Persist the bootstrap specification and seed before unblinding.

Predeclare fixed class calibration bins and major subgroups (competition with expected >=100, lead-time groups, recorded as-of quality groups), multiplicity handling and noninferiority tolerances. Proposed subgroup Brier harm tolerance +.01; require simultaneous upper intervals below that tolerance for prespecified sufficiently powered major groups. Sparse groups are explicitly unresolved, with narrower deployment scope considered only in a separately reviewed new protocol. Calibration ECE is diagnostic; no material new class bias >3 percentage points without investigation. Report reliability intervals and all n rather than selecting favourable bins. Public selected-market grades must not materially deteriorate; this requires a separately predeclared grading endpoint and margin, not comparison with 1X2 accuracy. These statistical and grading details are unresolved protocol work, so no confirmatory study may start yet.

## Sample size, operations and next approval

The current original-versus-market paired Brier SD is .18461. With 80% power and one-sided 97.5% threshold, detecting mean change -.01 requires about 2,675 independent pairs; -.005 about 10,700; -.002 about 66,874. A planning dependence factor of 2 doubles those values. Candidate-relative variances and expected effects may differ substantially. Recalculate using independent development data for both primary comparisons and choose the larger feasible target **before freeze**; allow for the probability of meeting material point-estimate gates. Consider log-loss/subgroup precision too. If neither budget nor throughput supports the target, label a smaller exercise a feasibility pilot, never a powered accuracy claim.

No spending or storage limit is increased. Confirm projected receipt/raw history/manifests/indexes/operational growth against the existing 250 MB budget; counts of shadow probability rows alone are not a storage forecast. Monitor budget/quota failures and pause private collection at its approved ceiling without altering public predictions. Do not discard unanalysed study evidence to make room. Back up private research before cleanup; test restoring schema, roles, hashes, snapshot links and operational failures in isolation. Retention/export must preserve the locked evaluation sample and contain no public credentials.

AI remains outside the primary study. Future provider adapters would use one normalized probability contract, model/version tagging, immutable evidence references, content-addressed cache keys including issue/evidence/config hashes, fail-closed daily/monthly spending caps, and a disabled-by-default shadow-only execution flag. Provider mocks/synthetic responses suffice for preparation; no calls, provider keys, paid resources or activation are introduced. Any AI candidate needs its own future freeze and budget approval.

Minimum next steps: review this research, choose a defensible single hypothesis, resolve inference/grading/power feasibility, and arrange a separately approved receipt-capable private evaluation environment. Supabase Auth/JWT/RLS/PostgREST/Edge Runtime and private publisher compatibility remain unverified on a full hosted stack; isolated PostgreSQL tests from PR #1 do not certify them. Do not merge/deploy either PR or modify either existing Supabase project to start this study without explicit approval.
