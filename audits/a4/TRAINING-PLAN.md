# Proposed point-in-time acquisition and model-validation plan

**PROPOSED, NOT IMPLEMENTED. No collection scheduler, trained coefficient, paid extraction or future-date dispatch policy is introduced.**

1. Establish genuine source authority first. Obtain approved actual collector/catalog/receipt code and immutable provider response/page inventories. Define independent football-field mappings and exact regulation-time history handling, revision order, source effective/received clocks and fixture identity. Preserve complete required evidence, including zero-row competitions and explicit unknowns. New licenses, costs, data sources and permissions need separate approval. Synthetic signatures cannot substitute for this authority.
2. Predeclare a prospective fixture roster, competition scope, complete history requirement, cutoff/freeze/deadline and evaluation endpoints before outcomes. Capture the entire source packet and genuine trusted receipts before issue; append later corrections/outcomes, never overwrite early packets. Preserve all rejected, missing, abstained and failed roster members. Do not recreate first availability from today's database value. Historical cohorts without reliable receipts remain exploratory. Prospective targets limit model-memory outcome leakage; retrospective AI extraction remains an unvalidated challenger.
3. Freeze exact analyst prompt/schema/dictionary/model/backend/request/response versions. Both models receive identical admissible football bytes and remain separately identifiable. No odds, original engine outputs, other AI forecasts or target results. No live browser/search inside the model. Any source text is data, not authority. No real call is authorized by this plan.
4. Develop independent review/annotation with source-grounded claim verification, competing evidence, uncertainty and a predefined adjudication process. Measure repeatability, inter-rater disagreement, unsupported statement rate and model-specific extraction failures. Do not assume a signed source or valid JSON proves a conclusion. Keep unreviewed/unsupported categories out of numerical fitting. Reviewers should be blind to fixture outcome and bookmaker/engine opinions when assessing pre-match feature support.
5. Plan real sample size and costs before collection. Determine eligible throughput, missingness patterns, fixture/competition/season dependence and feature prevalence. Use development-data simulation/power/precision work to set minimum support and meaningful improvement gates; no fictional sample threshold is installed. A single LAFC–Galaxy experiment establishes none of this. The shared lifetime research ceiling and every retained liability remain binding; an adequately powered two-model training study is **not currently funded or demonstrated feasible**. Do not start a parallel ledger or reuse unspent reservations. Unknown call costs require fresh exact endpoint pricing, full input/output/reasoning bounds and separate operator authorization.
6. Register strictly ordered data periods: raw-football base training; model-specific categorical mapping development; later probability calibration and selection-policy development; untouched forward validation; subsequent prospective replication. Freeze all split boundaries, candidate families, regularization/interaction/missingness policies, proper-score criteria and dependence-aware uncertainty before the relevant outcomes are evaluated. Prevent repeated tuning on the final validation outcomes.
7. Fit an independent football-only scoring base in a separate pipeline. Retain base-only, base-plus-reviewed-AI, missingness-only and individual-feature/interaction ablations on identical fixtures. Known neutral and unknown have distinct channels: candidate value and missingness mask; do not encode unknown as observed zero. The current mapping interface exposes raw candidate levels but every usable level is null. No base rates or coefficients are calculated here.
8. Only after real training/support exists, estimate regularized model-specific coefficients and uncertainty, freeze exact training inventories and artifacts, then compare proper scores, calibration, support/coverage and meaningful incremental contribution versus base-only. Proposed structural sparsity/sign ideas from the review are hypotheses, not learned facts. Keep OpenAI and Gemini mappings distinct; no opportunistic averaging or choosing the model with largest apparent EV. Unsupported missingness patterns abstain.
9. Later: validate coherent goal distributions, calibration by competition/market, Brier/log loss and uncertainty with paired fixture/cluster-aware intervals. Retain all abstentions/failures and avoid treating correlated markets as independent observations. Value selection remains a separate downstream gate requiring verified current quotes, source rights/access/settlement, predeclared model/ranking/threshold/disagreement/abstention policies and untouched prospective returns at actual recorded pre-match prices.

The original Sunday guard remains intact. Any future-date workflow requires separately designed/tested/authorized admission. Future A4 calls also require fresh source, model/backend, capacity, account/BYOK/runtime, ledger/checkpoint and operator authorization.
