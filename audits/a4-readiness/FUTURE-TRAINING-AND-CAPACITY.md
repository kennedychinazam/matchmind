## Source and cohort first

Use genuine pre-cutoff source receipts and immutable full inventories; distinguish an accepted prior research packet from new A4 authority. Establish regulation-time labels, measured-stat semantics, source catalog mappings and complete required history. Raw field names or a later database value do not establish historical availability. Do not copy the whole raw fixture/source object to the independent analyst: existing sources can also contain odds or engine outputs outside the football rows.

Freeze target scope, history/field requirements, cutoff/issue/deadline policy, exclusions, dictionary, prompt and model versions before collecting a cohort. Retain missing/rejected/abstained/failed roster members. Measure coverage per field/window, both teams, competition/season and feature; some xG is not a complete recent comparison. A source-bound label does not prove a player absence, tactical style, complete schedule or opponent-strength adjustment.

The original execution-date guard still applies; an actual new future-date workflow needs separate design/tests/authorization. Do not infer today's throughput from a small earlier roster. For a genuinely admitted stream, let lambda be observed eligible fixtures per week after source, field, time, capacity, review and budget gates. Until those gates exist the currently certified stream can be zero. Duration planning is N/lambda; an unknown lambda cannot produce a credible calendar estimate.

## Independent mapping and evaluation

Keep the independent raw-football statistical base, each model's categorical outputs, missingness and a base-only comparator separately frozen. Human/source-grounded semantic review comes before numerical feature admission. Unknown is not observed zero. Compare base-only, model-specific added features, missingness-only and feature-group ablations on identical later fixtures. Do not automatically average models, choose the larger apparent EV or treat agreement as certainty.

Use ordered base-training, feature-mapping development, calibration/policy development and untouched forward validation periods. Register target metrics, meaningful improvement/nondegradation, support/missingness, model family/regularization and uncertainty methods before outcomes are examined. Evaluate proper scores and calibration with fixture/team/time/competition dependence and the same selection coverage/abstentions. Multiple markets on one match are not independent samples. Old A3 experiments do not become A4 training forecasts by relabeling.

No fixed sample requirement is installed. One planning approximation for one paired score comparison is:

`N_eff approximately ((z_alpha + z_power) * sigma_paired_difference / minimum_meaningful_delta)^2`.

For a hypothetical two-sided 5% comparison and 80% power, the squared z-sum is approximately 7.84. Hypothetical sigma/delta pairs 0.10/0.02, 0.20/0.02 and 0.20/0.01 give approximately 196, 784 and 3136 effective fixtures. These are **sensitivity examples**, not measured variances, recommended thresholds or sufficient training sample sizes. Dependence, rare features, missingness, hyperparameter selection, multiple models/markets and separate training/calibration periods increase requirements. Genuine pilot variance, prevalence, dependence and justified practical effect must determine a registered design, ideally with dependence-aware simulation/learning curves before paid scale-up.

## Capacity

Measure the complete required evidence, prompt, dictionary, schema, visible output, reasoning and framing separately. The existing synthetic request's bytes are not a real full-history token count. Current exact-model tokenization, gateway translation and output/reasoning behavior need verification; do not substitute another model's tokenizer or assume A3 output/pricing bounds transfer. Public structured-output claims do not prove acceptance of the unchanged schema or semantic accuracy.

Minify only non-string whitespace. Lossless columns/rows or identifier dictionaries must preserve every required row, field, order, number/null/type, source reference and receipt, with decoder equality checks and immutable original bytes retained. Dictionary/schema/prompt changes require proper versioning and provenance; do not silently replace the accepted v0.2.0 packet grammar. Removing history, rounding statistics or sending summaries instead of required originals is not lossless. HTTP compression does not establish token savings.
